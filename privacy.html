Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

The Daily Dev Hacks Uploader ("the Service") is a personal tool. This policy explains what data it handles when you connect a TikTok account.

Data we receive from TikTok

  • Basic profile info (user.info.basic): open ID, display name and avatar. These are used only to confirm which account is connected.
  • An OAuth access token and refresh token. They are used only to upload draft videos (video.upload) to your TikTok inbox.

How the data is stored

Tokens and profile info stay only on the creator's own computer. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. No analytics or tracking cookies are used, and this website stores no personal data.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the connection is active. Revoking access in TikTok (Settings → Security & permissions → Apps and services) invalidates the tokens, and the local copies are then deleted.

Children

The Service is not directed at children under 13.
